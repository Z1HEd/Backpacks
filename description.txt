Adds 5 Types of backpacks, each crafted out of their respective Hyperfabric.
Fabrics are crafted using a Spindle And Distaff (2 Sticks, 2 Hypersilk, 1 Rock), and backpacks are crafted using a Weaving Loom (3 Sticks, 2 Wood, 1 Iron Bar).
Also adds a new material: Cosmic Bars, crafted from 2 Deadly Bars, 2 Solenoid Bars and 1 Midnight Wood.
Hyperfabric is crafted from 2 Hypersilk and 1 Stick. All other hyperfabrics are crafted from 2 of the previous type plus 2 of its material (except Cosmic Hyperfabric - it only takes 1 Solenoid Hyperfabric and 2 Cosmic Bars).
The types of backpacks are:
- Backpack - 1/4 chest capacity
- Refinforced Backpack - 1/2 chest
- Deadly Backpack - 1 chest
- Solenoid Backpack - 1.5 chest
- Wormhole Backpack - 1 chest.
Solenoid Backpack can be activated with RMB. It will then attract nearby items like Solenoid Collector. Also when activated all gathered items are automatically stored in it.
Wormhole Backpack acts as an ender chest - one inventory per player. Losing it wont destroy its contents - craft a new one and it will link to the same inventory.
Wormhole Backpack is the only backpack that can store other backpacks (except other Wormhole Backpacks).
